# Makespace storage terminal

This is a handheld device for scanning Makespace storage barcodes and
performing some operations based on them.

## Actions

 - scan a barcode and describe the thing it is on

 - scan a series of barcodes, of which the last is a location, to
   indicate that all the other items are now in that location

## Design

A handheld computer with wifi, with a barcode scanner, display, and
keypad, and a battery and wireless charging receiver.
